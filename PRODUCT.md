# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite + TypeScript (user's choice), static build deployed by GitHub Actions to GitHub Pages at
`https://ron997.github.io` (existing repo `ron997/ron997.github.io`, user site, so `base: '/'`).

## Users

1. **Primary — ML/AI hiring teams.** Recruiters and engineering managers filling Data Scientist, ML engineer,
   and GenAI/agentic-systems roles. They skim many candidate links between meetings, usually on a laptop,
   sometimes from a phone via LinkedIn. Their job: decide within a minute whether Rounak is worth a call,
   then find a way to reach him or grab his resume.
2. **Secondary — consulting clients.** Founders and teams who need computer-vision, OCR/document-intelligence,
   or LLM-agent pipelines built. Their job: judge whether he has shipped this kind of system and start a
   conversation.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Rounak Burman, Data Scientist and ML engineer based in Kolkata, India. It turns the
resume into a site that is memorable enough to stand out from other ML portfolios while staying fast to scan.
Success: a hiring manager leaves knowing what he builds, believing he ships it, and having emailed him or
downloaded the resume.

## Positioning

He turns unstructured visual material (live video, scanned PDFs, photographs, fabric designs) into
structured, queryable data using computer vision, OCR, and LLM agents, and he ships those systems end to end
because he started as a full-stack developer (Curved Pixel, 2020–22) before his MS in Computer Science.
Range across CV (YOLOv8, DeepSORT, pose), document intelligence (Docling, Mistral OCR), and agentic LLM
orchestration (LangGraph) is the differentiator, backed by measured accuracy figures.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive from LinkedIn, the resume's GitHub link, or a direct share. Desktop first, phone second.
- The resume PDF is the artifact recruiters forward internally, so it must be one click away.
- Contact happens by email or LinkedIn.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single page, static, no backend. No forms that need a server.
- Public contact: email `rounak42.rb@gmail.com`, LinkedIn `linkedin.com/in/rounak-burman`,
  GitHub `github.com/ron997`, downloadable resume PDF.
- **No phone number on the page.** (The downloadable PDF includes it; the user approved that.)
- **Client work is text only.** TCG Digital, UMass Boston, Lynchval, and Curved Pixel work is described in words
  and resume figures. No visual recreations of client data, client pipelines, or client outputs.
- Content must be easy for Rounak to edit later (copy lives in one data file).

  brand identity.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with what he builds, proven by real numbers, not adjectives.
2. Capability may be demonstrated on his own material (his resume, his photo); client work stays in words.
3. Every page state answers "how do I reach him" within one scroll.
4. Fast and readable on a recruiter's laptop and phone; nothing waits on a long intro.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard reachable, visible focus, contrast ≥4.5:1 for body text, reduced-motion honoured for
scroll effects as well as entrances.
